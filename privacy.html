curomee Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Quattro Gatti (the "Developer") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how user information is handled in the desktop application "curomee" (the "App").

1. Information We Record and Where It Is Stored

The App detects when a cat steps on your keyboard (multiple keys pressed at the same time) and keeps a history of these detections. The following information is recorded:

  • The date and time of each detection, and information about the keys involved
  • How long the App has been running (used to calculate CPH, our measure of detection frequency)
  • Your App settings (such as detection sensitivity and notification settings)

All of this information is stored only on your computer (in the App's local data folder). It is never sent to the Developer's servers or to any external service.

2. Personal Information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location. No account registration is required.

3. "Research Together" Feature (Planned)

We plan to offer a "Research Together" feature in the future, in which users may voluntarily take part. This feature will collect data only from users who have given prior consent, and the data will be used for research on cat behavior. Detailed handling policies for this feature will be provided by updating this Policy when the feature launches.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not provide or sell information obtained through the App to any third party.
